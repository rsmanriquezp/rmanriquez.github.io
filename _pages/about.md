**Rodrigo Manriquez Peralta (1992)**. I am a double PhD student in Artificial Intelligence at Vrije Universiteit Brussel (VUB) and in Psychology and Neuroscience at Maastricht University (MU), based at the [AI Lab in Brussels](https://ai.vub.ac.be/). I am interested in the intersection between AI and neuroscience, particularly in how biologically inspired models can be informative for understanding brain function and cognition. My current research focuses on modeling auditory processes in the brain using spiking neural network architectures.

My research interests include computational modeling of speech production and auditory perception, as well as AI methods applied to acoustics, speech, and neuroscience. I have expertise in signal processing digital hardware design, and algorithm development for biomedical and AI, and Neuro-AI applications.

I previously studied at Universidad Técnica Federico Santa María (Chile), where I obtained my Bachelor's degree in Electronics Engineering. During my Master's studies, I worked at the [Voice Production Lab](https://vplab.usm.cl/) in the same institution, focusing on topics related to speech and vocal production.
