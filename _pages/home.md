I am a double PhD candidate in Artificial Intelligence at Vrije Universiteit Brussel (VUB, Belgium) and in Psychology and Neuroscience in Maastricht University (MU, The Netherlands). 

I am interested in the intersection of AI and neuroscience, currently focusing on modeling auditory processes in the brain using spiking neural networks. Research topics include computational modeling of speech production and auditory perception, as well as AI methods applied to acoustics, speech, and neuroscience. Expertise in signal processing and algorithm development for biomedical and Neuro-AI applications.

Having previously worked with people from diverse fields—from clinicians to mathematicians—has given me a unique perspective on problem-solving in engineering and neuroscience. I thrive in multidisciplinary research environments.
